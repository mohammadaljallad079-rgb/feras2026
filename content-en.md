## 13. Privacy Policy (standalone page)
> Draft approved by the Owner in the specification (section 15). Legal review before publishing; the developer confirms the actual configuration (hosting, providers, storage, cookies) before launch.

**1. Data controller**

This policy explains how Start Up Sphere Holding W.L.L collects, uses and protects the personal data you provide when you use our website or submit a request, an opportunity or a CV. The company is registered in the Kingdom of Bahrain, commercial registration no. 165704-1. For privacy enquiries: info@sup-edu.com.

**2. Data we collect**

We collect the data you provide through our website forms, which may include: your name, email and contact number; the type of service requested and the request description; the project country when you submit an opportunity; the information and files you attach; the information contained in a CV or job application; and the data needed to operate and protect the website, if technical tools are enabled for that purpose.

**3. How we use data**

We use data to receive and respond to your request, route it to the appropriate party within the group, review commercial and investment opportunities, consider job applications, communicate about your submission, protect the website and comply with legal obligations. We do not use your data to send direct marketing messages except as legally required, and you may object to such use.

**4. Sharing data**

We may share the data necessary to handle a service request with the relevant group company. Authorized staff, technical service providers acting for the holding, or official bodies where the law requires may also access it. We do not sell your personal data.

**5. Retention**

We keep data for as long as needed to handle the request, opportunity or application, and for any other period required by law or needed to protect rights. Once the purpose ends, data is deleted, blocked or de-identified, as the law permits.

**6. Data protection and transfers**

We take appropriate measures to protect data against unauthorized access, use or disclosure. If we use a service provider or hosting outside the Kingdom of Bahrain, any transfer of data will be subject to the applicable Bahraini legal requirements.

**7. Your rights**

Subject to the law and the circumstances, you may ask to know what data we process, request correction of inaccurate data, object to certain processing, or request blocking or erasure where the conditions are met. To make a request, contact us at info@sup-edu.com; we may ask for proof of identity to protect your data.

**8. Cookies**

If the website uses cookies or analytics tools, we will explain their types and purposes in the website’s cookie settings and will ask for consent where it is required.

**9. Changes and contact**

We may update this policy when the website or the way we process data changes. The updated version will be published on this page with its update date. For enquiries or privacy requests, contact us at info@sup-edu.com.
